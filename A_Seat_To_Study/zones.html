Silent Zone

Dedicated to quiet focus and uninterrupted study

Quiet Environment

The Silent Zone is designed for students who require complete concentration without ambient conversation or distractions.

  • Suitable for individual study: Individual desks with personal power outlets and reading lamps.
  • Quiet environment: Maintained silence throughout the floor to minimize noise fatigue.
  • No group discussions: Voice calls, meetings, and group discussions are strictly prohibited.
  • Suitable for exam preparation: Ideal for semester finals, competitive exams, and thesis reading.

Collaborative Zone

Built for teamwork, discussion, and shared projects

Discussion Friendly

The Collaborative Zone brings classmates together in open seating and dedicated rooms without disturbing quiet readers.

  • Suitable for group study: Shared multi-seat tables and round team clusters.
  • Group discussions allowed: Speak naturally with teammates during active study sessions.
  • Team learning: Encourages peer mentoring, concept explanations, and study circles.
  • Collaborative assignments: Great for mini-projects, lab reports, and presentation practice.
