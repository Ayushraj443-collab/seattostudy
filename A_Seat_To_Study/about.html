About A Seat to Study

Project overview, problem statement, objectives, target users, and expected benefits.

Explore Study Spaces

1. Introduction

A Seat to Study is a college web application created to help students discover available library study spaces, check seat occupancy levels, distinguish between quiet and collaborative environments, and reserve individual seats conveniently.

Built using core front-end web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and browser LocalStorage), the platform provides a fast, practical, and easy-to-demonstrate workflow tailored for campus libraries.

2. Problem Statement

"Students often face difficulty finding suitable study spaces during busy academic periods. Some students prefer silent individual study, while others need collaborative spaces for group discussions. A Seat to Study aims to make finding suitable study spaces easier and more convenient."

Without visibility into floor-wise capacity and zone rules, students waste valuable study time searching across multiple library floors or unintentionally sitting in spaces mismatched to their work style.

3. Target Users

  • Individual students: Learners looking for quiet, distraction-free desks for daily reading and homework.
  • Students preparing for examinations: Candidates requiring dedicated silent rooms during mid-term and final exam weeks.
  • Group study teams: Classmates working together on assignments, lab projects, and seminar presentations.
  • Library users: Any college student visiting the library who wants to check occupancy before choosing a floor.

4. Project Objectives

  • Make study-space discovery easier: Centralize all floor and room details in one accessible portal.
  • Improve seat availability visibility: Display total, available, and occupied seats with clear occupancy percentages.
  • Separate silent and collaborative study areas: Clearly organize spaces into Silent Zones and Collaborative Zones.
  • Simplify seat booking: Let students pick seat numbers visually and prevent overlapping double-bookings.
  • Improve the student study experience: Reduce stress, save walking time, and gather continuous student feedback.

5. Expected Benefits

Time Savings

Students check open seats from their laptop or phone before entering crowded floors.

Better Noise Management

Explicit Silent and Collaborative zone labels guide groups and solo readers to the right room.

Fair & Transparent Access

Duplicate booking checks and easy cancellations keep seat availability accurate for everyone.

Data Transparency Notice: All study space seat counts, demo user accounts, sample bookings, and illustrative feedback entries in this website represent simulated prototype sample data for educational demonstration, rather than collected empirical survey data.
