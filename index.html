Welcome to A Seat to Study

A Seat to Study is a student-focused web platform designed to make locating, checking, and reserving study seats inside the college library effortless. Instead of walking from floor to floor with heavy backpacks, students can verify open seating before arriving.

Whether you need a quiet corner for exam preparation or a discussion table for a group project, the platform organizes every library floor and room clearly.

Problem Statement

"Students often face difficulty finding suitable study spaces during busy academic periods. Some students prefer silent individual study, while others need collaborative spaces for group discussions. A Seat to Study aims to make finding suitable study spaces easier and more convenient."
